Looklook desktop 1.60.0: all AI settings move to one "AI assistant" page, chats can have your own pet, and AI sessions come back on their own if every session drops at once.

- New: the "AI assistant" page (robot icon on the left; under "Tools" on a phone) has four parts: accounts & models, away from your desk (notifications, Feishu), tools & permissions (MCP, safety net) and pet, each showing how far it's set up. Settings now keeps only general, terminal, network & access and about; the start page has shortcuts to these four too.
- New: chat pets. Import a pet pack (.llpet / .zip) and a companion in the chat corner thinks, works, waits for your approval, celebrates and sighs along with the AI, and reacts when you click or drag it; installed Codex pets work too. Looklook ships no pet artwork; the pack format and a how-to guide are public. The pixel fish is gone.
- Improved: if every session drops at once (for example someone ran tmux kill-server in a terminal), AI sessions restart and continue their conversations automatically.
- Improved: the auto-stop for sessions you haven't opened in a while now skips sessions that still produce output, so ones you chat with from Feishu or that the AI keeps working in aren't stopped.
- Fixed: on phones, the System, Tasks and session list pages jumped up and down while scrolling.
- Fixed: the chat view went blank while Claude was replying, and could make the client quit.
- Fixed: swiping down to close a bottom menu on a phone triggered pull-to-refresh.
- Fixed: on the Mac app, Chinese punctuation with WeChat and similar input methods still needed two key presses.
