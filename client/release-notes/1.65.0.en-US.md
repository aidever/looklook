Looklook desktop 1.65.0: livelier pets that can live on your desktop and be imported from your other computers; pick skills and reference files in chat like in the terminal.

- New: pets understand what the AI is doing (thinking, running tests, committing, failing) and say a line after each round without spending tokens; pet them, click them, drag them anywhere on the page, and build a bond.
- New: in the desktop app the pet can live on your desktop and be dragged around even when the main window is minimized.
- New: Settings → Pets → Import from another computer: pets installed on your other computers can be imported here with one click; they download and install in the background, even if you leave the page.
- New: type $ in a Codex chat to pick a skill, just like in the Codex terminal.
- Improved: the chat input has @ (reference a file) and skill buttons, handy on phones; / commands at the start complete even when text follows.
- Fixed: "Choose from computer" for pet packs failed with "must be a full path" for files in the home folder.
