Looklook desktop 1.67.1: support for two-step verification on your account.

- New: if your account has two-step verification on, changing your password or creating an install key under "Account" asks for a code: the 6 digits from your authenticator app, or a recovery code.
- Improved: two-step verification errors (wrong code, expired verification, confirm it's you) now show clear messages instead of "Unknown error".
