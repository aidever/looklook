Looklook desktop 1.69.0: no more blank screen when opening remotely, phones pick up where they left off after the background, and a big Files upgrade.

- Improved: opening remotely shows the layout and connection progress right away, with what you saw last time; when it can't connect it tells you why (an offline computer shows when it was last online, and you can open another online computer directly). Pull to refresh works at any time.
- Improved: during disconnects and reconnects your content stays on screen with a small status note at the top; sending is paused and your draft is kept.
- Improved: when a phone comes back from the background, the connection is checked and rebuilt right away if needed, and terminals are kept for 10 more minutes; keys typed before the connection is confirmed are dropped after a moment, with a notice.
- Improved: the phone's back button closes dialogs, previews and multi-select first instead of leaving; the desktop app supports back/forward, and common shortcuts work from inside the terminal.
- New: Files opens on Quick access: files changed in the last 24 hours, projects, favorites and common places, plus searching all projects by file name.
- New: move and copy files (pick them, then tap "Put here" in the target folder); deleting goes to a trash you can undo; folders and multiple files download as one zip; paths can be sent to a chat.
- New: edit and save text files (you're warned if the AI changed it meanwhile); select lines and tap "Ask AI"; "Follow" a log as it grows; use the system share sheet on phones.
- New: "Keep screen on" in the More menu of a session on phones.
- Improved: your password, two-step verification and signed-in browsers are now all managed on the Looklook website, so you can change them even when your computers are offline. The Security section of the Account page shows whether two-step verification is on and opens the website in one click.
- Improved: when a Claude chat runs out of quota it now waits for the reset and continues by default, and isn't closed for being idle while waiting.
- Fixed: several things in Claude chats after the switch to the new mode: account error messages, scheduled tasks, /model and /rewind.
- Fixed: after /new reopened with the default account, the session still showed the old conversation's name.
