Looklook desktop 1.72.0: pick up a chat in the terminal, plus more accurate usage and prices.

- New: opening the Terminal tab in a Claude chat continues the same session in the terminal (after the current turn), and the chat page follows along live. Send a message from the chat to take it back automatically.
- New: account usage refreshes on its own (every 5 minutes for third-party APIs, every 10 minutes for official accounts).
- New: bars in AI stats are clickable: see a day's details and drill down by hour. Added a "Refresh prices" button.
- Improved: prices come first from the price table built into your local Claude Code, so new models are priced right away. Stats now include fast mode, 1-hour cache writes, long context, web search and advisor costs, closer to your real bill. New models without a price are estimated from their family and marked as estimates.
- Improved: more third-party "quota used up / expired" messages are recognized, and reset times are read from the message. When none is given, retries back off gradually and the time is marked as a guess. Expired quotas don't auto-continue.
- Improved: sessions are sorted by latest activity, like other AI coding clients.
- Improved: the pet now wanders up and down too, not just sideways.
- Fixed: answering a question or approval in chat seemed to do nothing until clicked several times; it now shows "Sent" right away.
- Fixed: picking a skill in a Claude chat showed raw <command-message> markup; it now shows as /skill args.
- Fixed: "Using Bash…" stayed on screen after pressing Stop; it now shows "Stopping…" and clears once stopped.
