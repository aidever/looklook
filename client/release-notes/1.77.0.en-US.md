Looklook desktop 1.77.0: Feishu / Lark notifications and the Feishu bot are now separate, and the bot is the assistant: DM it or @ it in a group to talk to the assistant.

- Feishu notifications now sit with WeCom, DingTalk, ServerChan and the other channels (AI → Remote & notifications), with their own "Feishu notifications" switch. Notifications always go to your DM; project groups are no longer created.
- The Feishu bot now talks straight to the assistant: DMs and @-mentions in groups go to the assistant, just like typing on its page. Replies stream in DMs; in groups you get one message when the answer is done. The bot's switch is in the assistant page's side panel.
- Someone else's question in a group: quote their message and @ the bot, and the answer @-mentions them. People you @ in your message are mentioned too; follow-ups that quote the bot's answer keep mentioning them; say "don't @" to skip. Only you can call the bot; it ignores others.
- When work you asked for in Feishu finishes, the update goes back where you asked (mentioning the same people in groups); approvals the assistant needs are sent to your DM.
- Guided Feishu CLI setup: the first time you message the bot, or when you send "help", a card walks you through installing it, creating its app and signing in with your Feishu account. The links work right in Feishu on your phone; the assistant page has the same steps. Once set up, the assistant can read and write your docs, calendar, Base and chats as you, and asks before sending anything.
- Removed: the "current session", /new project picker, replying to a card to continue a session, automatic project groups, forward routing and the bot menu. Picking the project and whether to start fresh is now the assistant's job. Old project groups stay; the bot ignores them unless @-mentioned.
