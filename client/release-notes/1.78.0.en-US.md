Looklook desktop 1.78.0: no more waiting on the assistant. Say several things in a row, and anything that takes a while runs in the background. The page is now the conversation on the left and "Activity" on the right.

- No waiting: anything that takes a while to look into (several repos, running tests, web research, tracking down a cause) goes to a background errand. The assistant replies in a line and is free for your next message, then reports back when the errand is done.
- Activity on the right: sessions the assistant started and its errands, grouped as Needs you / Working / Done, with a one-line result for finished ones. Click any of them to open it right there: read it, reply, or stop it without leaving the assistant. The panel can be hidden and remembers that; on phones, open it from "Activity" in the header.
- Sessions the assistant starts now get the assistant's permission level by default (switch at the top of Activity; applies from the next hand-off). If you say "let it go ahead" or "look only", your words win.
- New key or new login: the assistant moves to the current default account by itself. If it hasn't caught up, press ↻ in the header to force a restart. A message that stopped on an account error is answered once it's fixed.
- Switching engine (Claude Code ↔ Codex) or provider (official ↔ third-party) carries the conversation over instead of losing it.
- Opening a session the assistant started shows why it was sent there, with a link back to the assistant.
- Switching accounts from the usage panel after hitting a limit now continues the work automatically.
