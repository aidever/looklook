Looklook desktop 1.79.0: the assistant can wait on other people for you, and pull-to-refresh on phones no longer fires by accident.

- Waiting on others: tell the assistant "when X finishes this, continue with …" and it keeps track (the "Waiting" list in the assistant's Activity panel). When that person @-mentions the bot in the Feishu group, or a remote branch gets new commits, the assistant wakes up and carries on — or asks you first if you prefer. If the deadline passes it only tells you; it never chases other people.
- Sessions the assistant started can now tell it when they're stuck or need a decision, and after each turn the assistant sees the files actually changed and the commits made, not just the session's own summary.
- If a delegated session waits for your approval over 10 minutes, or makes no progress for 20 minutes, the assistant reminds you once.
- When someone @-mentions the bot in Feishu about something the assistant isn't waiting for, it's forwarded to you in a direct message.
- Phones: dragging the pet, a divider or a notification card no longer triggers pull-to-refresh; sideways swipes, swiping up then down, pinching and selecting text don't count either, and a "Refreshing…" badge shows after you let go.
